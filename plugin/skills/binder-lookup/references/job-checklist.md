# What each job should have

"What am I missing?", "how's the job?", "close the job" and the Job Tracker's Needs column all use
this. It's reminders only, never advice.

## The stage

Check in this order, and stop at the first that fits:
1. In Finished Jobs: **finished**
2. His final invoice in 7: **billing**
3. Any paper in 2 Payments: **in progress**
4. An estimate or contract in 1: **waiting on the deposit**
5. Otherwise: **new**

## What's due by each stage

Each stage also needs everything in the stages above it.

| Stage | What should be there by then | Folder |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting on the deposit | a signed estimate or contract | 1 |
| In progress | a deposit | 2 |
| | a building permit | 3 |
| | his insurance certificate | 5 |
| Billing and finished | a lien waiver from each supplier he owes (see below) | 7 |
| | his final invoice to the customer | 7 |
| | the warranty | 8 |
| | the final payment | 2 |

**How to check:**
- **Lien waivers:** only from suppliers he owes: every Who with a **Bill** in 4, and every Who with a
  **Receipt** charged to his account (open that receipt once to check). A receipt paid on the spot,
  like a hardware store run, needs no waiver. Match each of those names with a lien waiver from that
  supplier in 7. One waiver per supplier.
- **Final payment:** a Payment in 2, dated on or after his final invoice. A deposit never counts.
  - In "what am I missing?" and "how's the job?", it's a gap only 14 or more days after the invoice.
  - When closing a job, it's a gap at any age.
- **Finished jobs** are checked too.

## Money reminders (never advice)

- **No deposit yet** on a job that's waiting on the deposit: "no deposit yet on the $11,400.00
  estimate".
- **An unpaid invoice:** a final invoice, but no final payment 14 or more days later.
- **Truck & Shop this month** (the calendar month): how many receipts and bills, and their total.

"How's the job?" and the tracker also show the deposit against the estimate, like "deposit $4,000.00 of
a $13,650.00 estimate".

## Dates

- **Permits:** a permit on an open job that runs out within 30 days, or already has. Read the expiry
  date on the permit.
- **Idle jobs:** an open job with no final invoice yet, and no new paper in 14 days. Use the newest
  paper's date; photos count. A job with no papers counts from the day it started.
- **The Inbox:** papers waiting in the Inbox for 3 days or more.

## Not on the checklist

Only the papers in the table above count. Photos, an empty or missing folder on its own (including
4 Bills & Receipts), and amounts that don't add up are never gaps.
