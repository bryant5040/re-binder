Your R&E Binder

What it's for
Every paper for every job, in one place in your Google Drive.
Claude files them with clear names, finds them fast, and says what each job still needs.

How to put papers in
- Google Drive app: open “Inbox - Drop Here” in your binder. Tap +, then Scan or Upload.
- Or send a few photos in a Claude chat and say “file these”. After you tap Allow, tap each file
  Claude makes, then “Open in Google Drive”, and say “done”.
- iPhone: set Settings → Camera → Formats to Most Compatible, so Claude can read photos.

How filing works
1. Say “file my inbox”. Claude reads each paper and lists where each one goes.
2. Want a change? Just say it, like “number 3 goes to Martinez”.
3. Tap Allow (or say yes) and they're all filed. Tap Deny and nothing moves.
4. If Claude asks about a paper, like “is this Henderson or Martinez?”, just answer in a word.
Papers Claude isn't sure about, and personal papers, stay in your Inbox.

What to say to Claude (your own words work too)
- “start a job for Smith, 12 Elm St”: makes a new job's folders
- “file my inbox”: files your Inbox. For photos sent in the chat, say “file these”.
- “these go to Henderson”: tells Claude which job the papers are for
- “where's the Oak St lien waiver?”: finds any paper, with a link
- “how's the Henderson job?”: a short update on one job
- “what am I missing?”: what each job still needs
- “close the Henderson job”: says what's missing, then moves it to Finished Jobs
- “add a note to Henderson: customer wants gutters”: saves a dated note in that job
- “undo that”: puts your last filing back
- “set up my binder”: adds anything that's missing
- “how does my binder work?”: shows this again

Claude never
- moves or renames a paper without your OK
- deletes anything (anything taken out goes to Archived instead)
- changes what's written on a paper
- shares a file or contacts anyone
- touches anything outside your binder
- does anything by itself: you always ask first

Your Job Tracker
It's at the top of your binder: one row per job.
Claude makes a fresh one each time it changes anything in your binder.
